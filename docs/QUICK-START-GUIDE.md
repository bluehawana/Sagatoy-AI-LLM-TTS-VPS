# Sagatoy - Quick Setup Guide

## For Families: Get Your Toy Talking in 5 Minutes!

---

## What's in the Box

- ✅ Sagatoy plush toy with built-in speaker & microphone
- ✅ USB power cable
- ✅ This quick guide

---

## Step 1: Power On

1. Connect the USB cable to the toy
2. Plug the other end into a phone charger or computer USB port
3. The toy will turn on (you'll see a small light)

---

## Step 2: Connect to WiFi

### Option A: With Smartphone (Recommended)

1. **Download the Sagatoy App** (coming soon)
2. Open the app and tap "Add New Toy"
3. Follow the simple steps to connect to your WiFi

### Option B: With Computer

1. Connect toy to computer with USB cable
2. Open a web browser
3. Go to: `http://192.168.1.1`
4. Enter your WiFi name and password
5. Click "Connect"

---

## Step 3: Start Talking!

That's it! 🎉

The toy will connect to the internet automatically. Now you can:

- **Ask questions** - "What's the weather?"
- **Tell stories** - "Tell me a story!"
- **Learn things** - "Why is the sky blue?"
- **Sing songs** - "Sing me a song!"

The toy understands English, Swedish, Chinese, and more!

---

## Troubleshooting

### Toy won't connect to WiFi
- Make sure your WiFi password is correct
- Move the toy closer to the WiFi router
- Check that your WiFi works with other devices

### Toy doesn't respond
- Check the light on the toy (should be on)
- Make sure WiFi is working
- Try turning it off and on again (unplug and plug back in)

## Important Notes

- ⚠️ **Adult supervision recommended** for children under 3
- 🔊 **Volume** can be adjusted in the app or with buttons on the toy
- 📶 **WiFi** must be 2.4GHz (not 5GHz) for best connection
- 🔋 **USB power** - any standard phone charger works
